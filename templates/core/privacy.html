Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026.

The short version

We store your email address, the decks and collection you upload, and the results of the simulations you run - on servers in Switzerland. We do not sell any of it, we do not advertise, and there are no analytics or third-party trackers on this site. You can download everything we hold at any time, and you can delete your account yourself. Both of those are buttons, not requests.

Email we send you

Only what the account needs: the message that confirms your address, password resets, and - rarely - a notice about a change to these documents or to a plan you pay for. No newsletter and no marketing, ever. The mail is sent through a mailbox at Infomaniak, in Switzerland.

No analytics, no advertising, no error-reporting service and no third-party scripts. If we ever add a service that processes anything about you, it is named here before it is switched on.

Cookies

Three, all strictly necessary, none tracking you - which is why there is no consent banner:

  • • sessionid - keeps you signed in. Until you sign out, at most 2 weeks.
  • • csrftoken - stops other websites from submitting forms in your name. One year.
  • • messages - carries a one-line notice such as "deck imported" to the next page, then disappears.

Nothing is kept in your browser's local storage. You can block cookies in your browser; signing in then stops working, and the rest of the public pages still do.

How it is protected

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS, enforced). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Every account sees only its own data - that is checked on every request, not only in the menus. Sign-in, sign-up and password reset are rate-limited, and the pages carry a strict content security policy. Database backups stay with Infomaniak in Switzerland and are kept for at most 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you and get a copy, have it corrected, have it deleted, take it elsewhere in a machine-readable format, and - where we rely on a legitimate interest - object to the processing. Three of those are buttons, because a right that needs an email is a right with a queue in front of it:

  • • A copy, machine-readable: download everything as JSON, immediately.
  • • Deletion: delete your account yourself. It is immediate and cannot be undone. A running paid plan has to be cancelled first, so that Stripe stops charging you.
  • • Correction: decks, annotations and your collection are editable in the application.
  • • Everything else - including changing your email address - write to the contact address above. We answer within 30 days, and it costs nothing.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you live in the EU, to the data protection authority of your country.

A deleted account is gone from the live database at once. It survives only in backups until they age out (at most 30 days), and a backup is never used for anything but disaster recovery.

Decisions about you

None are automated. The simulator judges decks, not people: there is no profiling, no scoring of users and no decision with legal effect made by a machine.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If the change matters - a new service that sees your data, a longer retention period - we email account holders before it takes effect.
