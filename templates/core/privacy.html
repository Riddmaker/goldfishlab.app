{% translate "How it is protected" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS, enforced). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Every account sees only its own data - that is checked on every request, not only in the menus. Sign-in, sign-up and password reset are rate-limited, and the pages carry a strict content security policy. Database backups are encrypted before they leave the database server, stay with Infomaniak in Switzerland and are kept for at most 30 days. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} You may ask what we hold about you and get a copy, have it corrected, have it deleted, take it elsewhere in a machine-readable format, and - where we rely on a legitimate interest - object to the processing.   • • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}A copy, machine-readable: download everything as JSON, immediately.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Deletion: delete your account yourself. It is immediate and cannot be undone. A running paid plan has to be cancelled first, so that Stripe stops charging you.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Correction: decks and annotations are editable in the application.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Everything else - including changing your email address - write to the contact address above. We answer within 30 days, and it costs nothing.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} If you think we handle your data wrongly, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you live in the EU, to the data protection authority of your country. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} A deleted account is gone from the live database at once. It survives only in backups until they age out (at most 30 days), and a backup is never used for anything but disaster recovery. {% endblocktranslate %}
