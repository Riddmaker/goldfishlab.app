{% comment %} Engines and synergies (Themen-Doc, Paket 1, PR 3), under the combos in "Combos & engines". Read from the deck's themes by `cards.synergy`, on every page load, free for everyone: no button, no run. An engine names its cards, its theme and level (the word, as on the chips), and one sentence made of what each card does. An engine a piece short says what is missing, with a dashed rule instead of a solid one. The pairs show the strongest few open and the next ones folded, each with its three numbers under "Advanced". The owner can lock or dismiss each one (PR 4, `decks/_mark.html`): a locked one comes first and stays, a dismissed one waits in a fold at the end, and one whose cards left the deck rests, grey (`decks/_gone.html`). The whole of this is swapped by htmx after each click. Under each engine, by which turn it runs in the last finished run (PR 5, `decks.engine_watch`); a complete engine the run did not measure says the next one will. Nothing for an engine a piece short or resting: it cannot run. Under its sentence, how it plays, in a sentence or two of the deck's summary (PR 6, `simulations.summary.engine_notes`), in the language it was written in; only for the engine it was written about. Takes: reading (decks.themes.Reading), engine_rows (engine, running, note), deck. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %}
