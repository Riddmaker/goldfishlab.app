What is supported

Any CSV or TSV export. Moxfield, Deckbox, ManaBox, Delver Lens, Dragon Shield, Archidekt — and tools nobody here has heard of. Columns are read by what they mean rather than by which site wrote them, and where that is not enough you are shown the columns and asked. It takes about thirty seconds and it only happens once per file.

Plain text — one card per line, with or without a leading quantity. 1x Sol Ring and 31 Swamp both work.

Spreadsheets are deliberately not accepted. An .xlsx file is a zip archive and an .xls file can carry macros; reading either would mean running a large parser over a stranger's upload for no benefit you would notice. Every tool that exports a spreadsheet also exports a CSV.
