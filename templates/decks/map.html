Why you are being asked

Most exports let you choose which columns to include, so the same site produces a different file for every person. Reading this one by guessing would not fail loudly — it would import the wrong cards and say nothing.

The case that proved it: an export with the quantity column left out once imported a 28-Swamp deck as a 28-card deck with one Swamp in it. Every test passed. That is why this screen will not let you past a quantity column it cannot find without you saying what to do about it.
