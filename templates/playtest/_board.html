{% comment %} The board. Rendered inside the page and swapped on its own by htmx, so the two can never show different games. Every button is a real submit inside a real form. `hx-post` is an enhancement on top: with JavaScript off the same POST goes through, the view redirects to the board, and the page re-renders. Nothing here is reachable only by script. Laid out like a card game on a table (PR 19, design "Board A"): a head with the turn, the life and the mode; a row of moves; the table as dark felt with the commander, the battlefield rows, the stacks and the hand as a fan along its edge; the journal as a parchment page beside it (below it on a phone). The game is played on felt, what happened is written on parchment. Free and strict look the same. Strict alone has what its rules need: the phases and Next phase, the floating mana, Mulligan and Keep. Free has Draw, New hand and Next turn (keys D, N, T, Ctrl+Z, static/js/playtest-keys.js), puts every card down for free, and its journal guesses the turns and notes the unusual moves. Strict writes the engine's log into the same page. `hx-sync="this:drop"`: every form in here syncs on the board, so a click while a request is in flight is dropped (a double click, phase 12 J30). The server locks the session as well, for the forms sent without JavaScript. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n l10n icons %}
