Every button is a real submit inside a real form. `hx-post` is an enhancement on top: with JavaScript off the same POST goes through, the view redirects to the board, and the page re-renders. Nothing here is reachable only by script. Laid out like a card game on a screen (Phase 9 F): a slim bar with the turn, the phases and the mana; the table with the commander, the battlefield rows and the stacks; the hand as a fan at the bottom. The log and the branching are folded, because they are what you look for, not what you look at. `hx-sync="this:drop"`: every form in here syncs on the board, so a click while a request is in flight is dropped (a double click, phase 12 J30).
