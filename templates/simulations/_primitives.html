{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The editor rebuild: what the engine plays of this card, one building block at a time. Each block says who decided it - the card, you, a built-in default - and can be changed, switched off, or taken back to the card data. Every action is a plain form post: no script is needed. "Please check" (card model phase 2): the card data changed under a correction, or no longer has the block it corrects. Keeping it, discarding it or taking it back is offered on the page of the correction's scope only, like "Back to the card data"; the other scope's page links there. A block whose boxes depend on its kind - when a land enters untapped - has the kind picked first, by a link; the form then keeps it (PR 7). {% endcomment %}
