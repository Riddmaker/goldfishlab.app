Casting order

What the engine reads

These numbers measure your mana and your curve, not your deck's plan

The simulation casts whatever it can afford, highest priority first. That default — ramp early, then card advantage, then the curve — is a reasonable guess for a good many Commander decks and actively wrong for any deck that needs to hold two specific cards until both are castable. The engine cannot read a plan off a card list, so this page is where the plan comes from.

One number per card

Higher goes first, 0 to 100. Leave a box empty and the engine's own rule applies — 40 minus the mana value, so cheaper things first. Lands are not listed: the engine never casts one. Sorted by what it will actually do today, so a plan that is wrong is visible rather than alphabetical.
